How this works
  1. Gear comes from Raider.io, plus the catalyst and crafted stats it can't see, which are stored in the repo. Manual runs can use a pasted /simc export instead.
  2. QE Live's Upgrade Finder runs in a headless browser, first for Heroic and then for Mythic. No sockets are added, and your equipped gear counts at max upgrade.
  3. The boss or dungeon each upgrade drops from comes from QE Live's item database.
  4. This page is republished after every run.
